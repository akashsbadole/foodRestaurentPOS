Quick Help
TCS/TDS Overview
  • TCS: Tax Collected at Source by seller
  • TDS: Tax Deducted at Source by payer
  • Tax deposit due by 7th of next month
  • PAN verification mandatory for deductions
Important Notes
  • Verify PAN before deduction
  • Maintain proper documentation
  • Deposit tax by due date to avoid penalties
  • File quarterly TCS/TDS returns
  • Issue Form 16/16A to deductees
